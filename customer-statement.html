Monthly Invoice & Final Billing Invoice

Create a single-month invoice first, then create a final billing invoice that keeps the selected billing month as current and adds only older unpaid months as pending balances.

Invoice Type:
Single Month Invoice: shows only the selected billing/delivery month. Use this to create August, September, October, etc. separately.
Final Billing Invoice: shows the selected month as the current billing month. Only earlier unpaid or partially paid months appear underneath as “Balance — Pending Month of …”. The selected billing month is never labelled as outstanding.
Select the customer, billing month, issue date and due date.
